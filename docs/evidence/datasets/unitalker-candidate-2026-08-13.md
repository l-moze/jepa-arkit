The repository describes the archive as a mixture of BIWI, VOCASET, MeshTalk/Multiface,
HDTF/RAVDESS-derived 3DETF data, FaceForensics++, in-house Chinese speech, and in-house song data.
The repository does not provide one archive-level license that establishes training and
redistribution rights for every upstream source. Its training instructions instead direct users to
the individual upstream datasets.

Consequently, downloadability is not treated as authorization. The archive remains in
`data/candidate/`, and the audit report must retain `ready_for_training=false` until every selected
sub-source has a saved rights profile and a verified annotation-to-canonical conversion.
