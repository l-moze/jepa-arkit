## Verified permissions and restrictions

The official page grants a personal, non-exclusive, non-transferable right to use and modify the
data/software for non-commercial scientific research, education, or artistic projects. It allows
derivative works within that scope. It prohibits commercial use, commercial services/products,
commercial neural-network training, and third-party redistribution without prior written
permission. Commercial licensing requires a separate request to the publisher.

## Project classification

- Research training: allowed after the account holder accepts the current terms.
- Research derivatives/checkpoints: allowed only on the research track and subject to the terms.
- Product training or product checkpoint ancestry: blocked without a separate commercial license.
- Dataset redistribution: blocked.
- Public benchmark disclosure: allowed for scientific publication with the required citation,
  but media/data redistribution remains blocked.

This evidence file records the public terms. It is not proof that a project account has accepted
the license or that the dataset has been downloaded.
