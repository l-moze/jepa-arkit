## Verified contents

The official record describes 24 professional actors (12 female and 12 male) performing two
lexically matched English statements. Speech covers neutral, calm, happy, sad, angry, fearful,
disgust, and surprise conditions. The speech video release contains 2,880 MP4 files: 60 trials,
two modalities, and 24 actors. Each actor is distributed in a separate
`Video_Speech_Actor_XX.zip` archive with an official MD5 checksum.

For the first JEPA-ARKit release, only the full audio-video speech files (`modality=01`,
`vocal_channel=01`) are eligible. Video-only duplicates, songs, and audio-only duplicates are not
ingested. This yields 1,440 candidate clips before quality filtering.

## Rights classification

The official record declares CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 and separately offers commercial licensing. This
project therefore classifies the open release as research-only. Attribution and share-alike
requirements apply to public derivatives. No RAVDESS-derived data, features, checkpoint, or
animation output may enter the product track without a separate commercial license and legal
review.

Downloading the open Zenodo release does not expand those permissions. The original archives are
kept out of version control, and every derived sample retains `source_id=ravdess_v1_0_0`, actor ID,
the source filename, archive checksum, and a withdrawal key.
